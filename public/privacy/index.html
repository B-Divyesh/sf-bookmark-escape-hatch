Privacy

Your bookmark files stay on your device.

What the app processes

Bookmark Escape Hatch reads the HTML, JSON, or CSV file you choose.

Parsing, reports, and downloads happen inside your browser. The app never uploads bookmark URLs, titles, tags, notes, or archive metadata.

Local storage

A completed real inspection is stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. This lets you restore it after a refresh.

Demo inspections use a separate database. “Start for real” clears the demo database before returning to the workbench.

Use “Clear saved inspection” to remove your real snapshot. Clearing site data also removes it.

Network use

There are no analytics, ads, third-party fonts, trackers, or remote content requests.

A service worker downloads the application files for offline use. Hosting systems may retain ordinary request logs without bookmark contents.

Your control

You can export the neutral archive and reports at any time. The generated files use JSON, HTML, and CSV.
